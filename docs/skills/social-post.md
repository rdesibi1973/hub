# Social posts (Facebook + Instagram)

Use when Roberto asks for social posts ("post Facebook", "post Instagram", "programma i post", blog of the week, photo post), or types /social-post.
Talk to Roberto in Italian; posts are in English.

Before every social session read the Project doc `claude/SOCIAL_PLAYBOOK.md` (account IDs, composer steps, past mistakes — it is the detailed reference; this file is the short checklist). Current calendar and texts: `claude/SOCIAL_FB_2026Q4.md` and `claude/SOCIAL_FOTO_POST_2026-10.md` — update them at the end.

## 1. Brand first (the most common mistake)

| Brand | What goes there | Hashtag |
|---|---|---|
| Savannah Explorers (FB + IG @savannahexplorers) | blog, safaris, sightings, guests on safari | only #SavannahExplorers |
| The Orangi Collection (FB + IG @theorangicollection) | anything about the lodges: Kifaru, Marera, Orangi River, Arusha Explorers Lodge | only #TheOrangiCollection |

- Never cross-tag the two brands. Never use the separate "Serengeti Kifaru Tented Lodge" page.
- Before clicking Schedule, check that "Post to" shows the right brand.

## 2. Cadence

- Tuesday 10:00 EAT: Savannah Explorers English blog article (savannahexplorers.net/blog), Facebook only. Texts from Stefano (mypresslab) or written by Claude from the article.
- Thursday 10:00 EAT: one photo/video post for Savannah Explorers and one for The Orangi Collection, FB + IG.
- Meta schedules only 20 minutes to 29 days ahead; beyond that save as draft and tell Roberto.

## 3. Material

- Photos/videos come from the WhatsApp group "Marketing Team The Orangi Collection"; Roberto saves them to `H:\My Drive\Social\<Savannah Explorers | The Orangi Collection>\NN_<Lodge/theme> - <description> (<date>)`.
- Look at every photo/video before writing (contact sheet, ffprobe for duration and orientation).
- Only the files to upload stay in the folder; photos with recognizable guests go in "Con ospiti - non pubblicare senza ok" and need Roberto's ok (guest consent).

## 4. Texts

- Facebook: narrative prose, 3–5 hashtags.
- Instagram: short caption + separate hashtag block; emoji allowed in posts.
- Nothing alarming for clients (malaria, mosquitoes, drowning stats, safety warnings).
- Location: if the exact park is not certain, just "Tanzania".
- Show Roberto the FB and IG texts and get his ok before scheduling.

## 5. Scheduling

Built-in browser, Meta Business Suite: follow `SOCIAL_PLAYBOOK.md` §4 step by step (upload by Roberto, IG ratio, Reels for vertical videos, dates then hours, "Schedule" never "Share now"). After scheduling, reload the Scheduled list and verify date, time, platforms and brand of every post.

## 6. Close

Tell Roberto in Italian what is scheduled (brand, platform, date/time, title) and anything left as draft. Update the calendar doc and add any new mistake to `SOCIAL_PLAYBOOK.md` §5.
