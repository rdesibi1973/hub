# Sales agent — exceptions

Not a skill: the special cases of `sales-agent` (same folder). The core skill points here ("exceptions §X"); read only the section it names. Each rule keeps its date and the request it came from. When a rule becomes the normal way of working, move it into `sales-agent.md`.

## §A. Hub and PC access

Hub access: a `403 {"ok":false,"error":"Forbidden"}` means the `X-Agent-Key` header is missing or wrong, not that the Hub is unreachable — send the key from `api.txt` before concluding the Hub can't be reached (5 Nov 2026).
The key (`api.txt`, Dropbox path `/Exchange/Savannah-Hub/hub/api.txt`) is read with the Dropbox connector: no access to the PC is needed. Don't use the Hub in the browser as a fallback: it asks for the login (22 Nov 2026, SophiaKusay; replaces the 20 Nov 2026 rule that the key could only be read from the PC).
Access to `C:\Dropbox` refused or not yet granted: the reply draft `.eml` is built in the workspace and sent to Roberto in the chat (SendUserFile), with the programme Word attached when it goes with the quote; as soon as the folder is connected, save it in `Exchange\Draft-mail\` and a copy in the request's `mails\` subfolder (`device_commit_files`) (23 Nov 2026, Cutter4paxFeb27).
Before writing a reply, read the agency's whole thread (Gmail `get_thread`) to find its latest message — the request may have changed since the last draft (23 Nov 2026, Cutter4paxFeb27: Luisa's Elewana Pioneer mail came after the Namiri draft). The Gmail connector gives no Message-ID: take In-Reply-To / References from the previous draft in `mails\` or `old\` (§I) and tell Roberto the draft hangs on an earlier message of the thread.

## §D. Calc: `fill_calc` limits and sheet edits

- Every day whose lodge you change or add (beach nights beyond the template too) needs `accommodation` (J): a number or a formula (`"=279"`, `"=(250+71)*$B$1"`); park fees in `park_fees` (F) / `park_fees_desc` (G) when they change. A "hotel changed, J cost left from the template" warning or a "Hotel without accommodation cost" error means the margin is wrong: fix it before showing prices.
- Safari nights 1–25 August: `"august_supplement": true` (35 pp per safari night, children and teens included, beach nights excluded, added to F9; 31 Oct 2026); `false` only if it really does not apply.
- Check `rows[]` of the dry run (B–K per day, `changed`) before the confirm.
- An activity left on a template row that now holds another day (e.g. lunch boxes on a Serengeti day, natural walk on the departure day): `amount: null` is refused, so write `"activity": {"amount": "=0", "desc": ""}` to zero it — otherwise its cost stays in the totals (3 Nov 2026, Daniela2paxAgo27).
- `fill_calc` writes neither jeep (D) nor drinks (E), and does not clear activities (H/I) left on a template row that now holds another day (`"activity": null` leaves them). When the trip has more days than the template, or days move: in the dry run check that every safari day has D/E and that each activity sits on its own day. If not, after the confirm fix the sheet XML (D `=$D$5*$B$5`, E `=($B$2+$B$3)*4`, move/clear H/I), update the cached values (row 30 totals, B7, B9, D9, B10, D10), check with a recalculation on a copy, then `read_calc` (17 Oct 2026, Del Fiandra Calc 05).
- Beach booked by the agency itself ("a ZNZ si arrangia l'agenzia"): start from the safari-only programme (e.g. `Simba`, not `BeachSimba`) and add the beach nights yourself as "<place> - own arrangement" (no J cost, no beach transfers) up to the departure day. `fill_calc` cannot clear labels (B) or dates of leftover template rows, so a Beach template with more beach nights than the trip leaves extra days and fails the check. When the safari gets shorter, the row that becomes a beach day keeps the template's jeep/drinks (D/E): clear them in the sheet XML as above (21 Oct 2026, AmedeoMarino).
- `fill_calc` `days[]` are positional from row 17: to change one day only, use `row_offset` (e.g. 5 = sixth day), not the date; an activity is written as `{"amount", "desc"}`. Agency extras outside the standard package (e.g. private jeep + park fees + drinks, or a hotel such as Airport Planet Lodge) are quoted at cost × (1 + the agency's markup: STO 18 %, STOSPEC 14 %), rounded up to 5 USD, as their own price item; 14 % for an STO agency only as an exception Roberto approves (22 Oct 2026, FabrizioFlorio; 20 Nov 2026).
- Day rows run 17–29 (row 30 = Totals) in the templates. A longer trip: add the missing rows before Totals in the sheet XML (copy the last empty day row, shift the rows below, update B7, B8 and the row-30 SUM ranges), wait for the Dropbox sync, then `fill_calc` with all the days from row 17 — it finds the Totals row itself (5 Nov 2026, Nannetti5paxLug27: 18 days, rows 17–34).
- Writing a file back to his PC with `device_commit_files`: give every new version its own staging name in outputs (e.g. `calc_v2.xlsx`, `calc_v3.xlsx`) and never reuse the name of a file already committed in the session — a reused name can write the OLD version to Dropbox. After the commit, stage the file again and compare the checksum (or `read_calc`) before the next Hub action (15 Nov 2026, Veronica5paxLug27).
- Fewer beach nights than the Beach template: `fill_calc` refuses (422 "No hotel on beach night") while template rows are left after the departure day. Confirm `fill_calc` on the rows that stay, then in the sheet XML make the departure row (label "Znz-Home", transfer in H/I, no J/K), clear A–K of the leftover rows and update the cached totals (H30, J30, B7, B9, D9, D10, B10, F9–F12); check with a recalculation on a copy, then `read_calc` (15 Nov 2026, Veronica5paxLug27).

## §E. Which price goes in F9, teen and child discounts

Which price goes in F9 (Price to customer):
- Direct clients: STANDARD mode (markup 25%, D1) → rounded D9 = rack price → F9 (and H9).
- Direct clients, special price or discount: only in the email, the programme shows the rack (`rules-client-documents` §1).
- Direct clients with lodge changes and a beach extension: F9 = components, e.g. `=3625+270+435+195+2010+210` — standard programme rack + lodge-upgrade supplement (cost difference × 1.25, rounded up to 5: Kifaru → Orangi Luxury, 2 nights = 270) + domestic flights at the pricelist sale price + beach hotel at its rack rate + August supplement (added by `august_supplement`).
- Agencies: rounded D10 at the agency's level (markup TO in D2: 18% STO for standard agencies such as the Promoservice ones; 14% STOSPEC for the partners set on stospec in the Hub, e.g. EKO) → F9. `fill_calc` does not change D2: when the markup differs from the template's, set D2 in the sheet XML after the confirm, with the cached values (B9, D9, D10, B10).
- STOSPEC programmes (a pricing mode, not an agency): D2 = 14% (GRP files too, 20 Nov 2026); rounded D10 → F9 only; delete H9/H10 and the I9/I10 labels.
- Child discount (under 12): 50% of the accommodation per person, NET of the concession fee (71 pp per night inside the parks, e.g. Serengeti, also when it is inside a lodge rate such as Kifaru / Orangi FB 527) and of the WMA fee (33 pp per night) — (Calc J column total − concession / WMA) ÷ pax (Roberto, 26 Nov 2026, OdysseeDuPapillon) — + the teen discount (park-fee difference, H13); on the beach the child gets only what the hotel contract gives (e.g. Mvuvi: 6–10 y.o. 30 USD per night, 11+ as adult); check that the beach hotel accepts children at all (e.g. White Dream: no guests under 15). One figure for all pax bases of a group (not one per base). Write it in H14 as a formula, rounded to a whole number so Calc, Word and email show the same figure (ITI prints no decimals), e.g. `=ROUND((J30-71*$B$1*3)/$B$1/2+H13,0)` for 3 Serengeti nights, label in I14; teen discount (park-fee difference) in H13 / I13; F10 `=F9*$B$1-H13-H14`, G10 "Inc teen + child discount" when the booking has teens/children (Roberto, 16 Nov 2026, FNOCT; replaces "50% of the price per person on the safari part").
- With teens / children a negative margin in B10 is expected and not a problem: the sheet costs them as adults, but they pay lower park fees and children less at the lodges (Roberto, 26 Nov 2026, OdysseeDuPapillon).
- Teens: AFTER the last `fill_calc` (it clears H6:I14), write the teen discount in H13 with the label in I13 (e.g. "teen discount (2 teens 13 y.o.)") and subtract it in F10 (e.g. `=F9*$B$1-H13*2`, with ZNZ transfers `=F9*$B$1+140-H13*2`), note "Inc teen discount" in G10. Keep cached values (edit the sheet XML, not via openpyxl).
