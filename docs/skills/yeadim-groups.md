## 1. Who and how

- From the 2027 season Roberto handles Yeadimtravel personally (quotes, changes, contact with Golan). Earlier files were handled by Anderson and Sultan (sellers).

- Golan's position (Oct 2026): he will not negotiate every quote; he expects our best price from the start. Never quote "high to leave room for a discount".

## 2. Fixed service standards (check in every quote, Calc, staff mail and program)

1. Lunch included on the arrival day AND on the departure day. Arrival day: lunch line or a FB lodge in Arusha (not HB); departure day: hot lunch (Arusha Explorers, 30 pp in the 2027 Calc). If the flight times make a lunch impossible, tell Roberto instead of dropping it.
2. Older travellers: request rooms close to reception / main areas at every lodge, above all at Ngorongoro Marera Mountain View (Karatu), where the cottages are spread out. Write it in the staff booking mail and in the lodge reservation remarks, naming the guests with walking difficulty when known.
3. Breakfast: fresh vegetables (tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers or a fresh salad) every morning. Ask each lodge in the reservation remarks; at the Orangi Collection lodges tell the lodge manager too.
4. Sundowner in the Serengeti, always for their groups: at Serengeti Orangi River Luxury Lodge or Serengeti Kifaru Tented Lodge, one sundowner with 1 drink per person. Put it in the Calc (activities), in the program and in the lodge booking.
5. Dietary / medical notes from the pax list (gluten-free, vegetarian-eats-fish, no spice, walking difficulty) go in the Calc and in every lodge request.

## 3. Ngorongoro – the 24-hour rule (plan it, don't discover it on safari)

- The NCA entry permit is valid 24 hours from the time the jeep enters the NCA gate. Overnight inside the NCA (Lion's Paw, The Rim, Embalakai…) and then driving to the Serengeti easily goes over 24 h.
- Over 24 h → extra 71 USD pp (clients AND TL) + the extra fees for each jeep and guide, even if the crater is not entered again. Fee amounts: from `get_rates`, never from memory.
- How to plan it (crater → Serengeti): morning activity in Karatu → enter the NCA late morning → crater game drive → overnight on the crater rim → next morning continue to the Serengeti, exiting at Naabi within 24 h of the entry time. The Karatu activity goes in the Calc and in the program.
- Before sending a quote with a night inside the NCA: write the gate times (entry, crater, exit at Naabi) day by day and check they fit in 24 h. If they don't, tell Roberto and propose the Karatu morning activity; don't change the program alone.
- If a group will knowingly exceed the 24 h, the extra must be in the Calc and in the price, and stated to Golan in the quote – never charged during the safari.
