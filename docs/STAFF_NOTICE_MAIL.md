## Why
Company mailboxes are work tools. The company checks them to make sure clients and suppliers are answered and bookings
are made and corrected on time, and to protect clients' data.

## What is checked all the time
For the mailboxes of the sales team, the booking team and the shared mailboxes (info@, safari@, operations@), the Hub
reads the **mail headers only**: sender, recipients, date, subject and thread references. It uses them to measure reply
times and to compare mails with open bookings. Text and attachments are not read in this check.

## What can be opened for a specific problem
An administrator can open the **text of one thread** of glady@ or operations@ when there is a specific reason, for
example a client complaint, a supplier dispute, a booking error, or an employee being absent. Each time, the
administrator writes the reason, and the Hub records who opened what and when. Mailboxes are never browsed in general.

## Replies from your mailbox
To keep a thread together, an administrator can reply from the mailbox that holds the thread, signing as "Booking Team".
The reply appears in that mailbox's Sent folder.

## Passports
Clients' passports received by email for a confirmed booking are read to fill the guest list for park permits. Only
image and PDF attachments of mails linked to that booking are used. The document is read by an automated service
(Anthropic's Claude, outside Tanzania and Mauritius), a person checks the result, and the data is kept only in the
booking's `passports/` folder and in its Calc.

## Personal use
Please do not use company mailboxes for private matters: anything in them may be seen under the rules above. Any
private message found by accident is not used.
