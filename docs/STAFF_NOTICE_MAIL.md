## Why
Company mailboxes are work tools. The company checks them to make sure clients and suppliers are answered and bookings
are made and corrected on time, and to protect clients' data.

## What is checked all the time
For the mailboxes of the sales team, the booking team and the shared mailboxes (info@, safari@, operations@), the Hub
reads the **mail headers**: sender, recipients, date, subject and thread references. It uses them to measure reply
times and to compare mails with open bookings. Text and attachments are not read in this automatic check.

## What can be searched and read
**Any company mailbox** (@savannahexplorers.com) can be searched and read, text and attachments included, by the
administrator, or by the administrator's AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude, outside Tanzania and Mauritius) on his
request, for work reasons: for example following a booking or a client, a complaint, a supplier dispute, an error, or
an employee being absent. Each time a mail is read, the reason is written, and the Hub records who read what and when.
Reading does not change your mailbox: mails stay as they are, unread ones stay unread.

## Replies from your mailbox
To keep a thread together, an administrator can reply from the mailbox that holds the thread, signing as "Booking Team".
The reply appears in that mailbox's Sent folder.

## Passports
Clients' passports received by email for a confirmed booking are read to fill the guest list for park permits. Only
image and PDF attachments of mails linked to that booking are used. The document is read by an automated service
(Anthropic's Claude, outside Tanzania and Mauritius), a person checks the result, and the data is kept only in the
booking's `passports/` folder and in its Calc.

## Personal use
Please do not use company mailboxes for private matters: anything in them may be seen under the rules above. Any
private message found by accident is not used.

## How long
Headers are kept for 12 months. The text of a mail is displayed when read and not stored by the Hub. The access
record is kept for 24 months.

## Your rights
You can ask what has been recorded about you and which mails of your mailbox were read, and you can ask for
mistakes to be corrected, by writing to info@savannahexplorers.com.
